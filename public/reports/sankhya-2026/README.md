# Sankhya 2026 sustainability evidence

`verification-and-technical-support.jpg` is the unchanged page 7 image supplied
by the project owner on 11 October 2026. It is an excerpt of the Sankhya 2026
Sustainability Report, not the complete report or a separate certificate.

The Achievements feature describes the supplied page's limited assurance of
the greenhouse gas inventory by Adyra ESG Technologies, in accordance with
ISO 14064-3:2019. Switch Climate Tech is credited for technical guidance.
The page limits assurance to the GHG inventory; it does not establish blanket
ISO certification of the festival or assurance of all report content.

Standard reference: [ISO 14064-3:2019](https://www.iso.org/standard/66455.html).
